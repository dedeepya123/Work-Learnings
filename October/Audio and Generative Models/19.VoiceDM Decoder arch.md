# VoiceDMDecoder Architecture

## Purpose

The VoiceDMDecoder is the generative component of VoiceDM.

The encoder answers:

```text
What should be spoken?
Who should speak?
What context should be followed?
```

The decoder answers:

```text
Generate the actual audio representation.
```

---

## Position In VoiceDM

Pipeline:

```text
Text
 ↓

VoiceDMEncoder
 ↓

cond
cond_paddings
 ↓

VoiceDMDecoder
 ↓

Audio Features [B,100,96]
 ↓

SoundStream Decoder
 ↓

Waveform Audio
```

---

# Key Takeaways

- VoiceDMDecoder is the generative component of VoiceDM.
- It contains a DiTDecoder and an 8-step DDPM sampling loop.
- The decoder starts from noise.
- Conditioning comes from the encoder (`cond`).
- DiT predicts denoising information at each timestep.
- Reverse diffusion progressively refines the representation.
- The final output is an audio feature tensor of shape `[B,100,96]`.
- The output is not waveform audio.
- SoundStream is responsible for converting these features into speech.
