# DiTBlock Architecture

## Purpose

A DiTBlock is the fundamental building block of the diffusion transformer.

Each block performs three operations:

```text
1. Self-Attention
2. Cross-Attention
3. Feed Forward Network
```

Unlike a normal Transformer block, DiTBlock is also conditioned on:

```text
Diffusion Timestep
```

through AdaLN-Zero.

---

## High-Level View

```text
Current Audio Features
          ↓

      Self Attention
          ↓

      Cross Attention
     (Conditioning)
          ↓

     Feed Forward
          ↓

 Updated Audio Features
```

---

```text

        ↓

```

Instead of:

```text

```

we have:

```text
One Model

Adaptive Behavior
```

# Key Insight

The decoder is not generating audio from text in a single step.

At every diffusion iteration:

```text
Current Audio Representation

        ↓

Self Attention
        ↓

Cross Attention To cond
        ↓

FFN
        ↓

Better Audio Representation
```

Repeated:

```text
12 DiT Blocks
```

inside a diffusion step,

and

```text
8 Diffusion Steps
```

overall.

So a text token may influence the generated audio **dozens of times** through repeated cross-attention interactions.
