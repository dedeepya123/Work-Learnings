# Decoder Mental Model

When thinking about VoiceDMDecoder, avoid thinking:

```text
Text
 ↓
Audio
```

because that hides what the model is actually doing.

A better mental model is:

```text
Conditioning Tensor
(Text + Speaker + Context)

          +

Noise

          ↓

8-Step Diffusion Process

          ↓

Audio Features

          ↓

SoundStream Decoder

          ↓

Waveform
```

---

## What Does The Decoder Receive?

The decoder receives:

```python
cond
```

which was produced by the encoder.

Conceptually:

```text
cond

=
Speaker Information
+
Audio Context Information
+
Text Information
```

Shape:

```python
[B,320,768]
```

This tensor describes:

```text
What to say
Who should say it
What context to follow
```

---

## How Does Generation Start?

Generation does not start from audio.

It starts from:

```text
Noise
```

Shape:

```python
[B,100,96]
```

Conceptually:

```text
Random Audio Representation
```

containing no meaningful speech information.

---

## Diffusion Process

VoiceDM performs:

```python
8 Diffusion Steps
```

The goal of each step is:

```text
Take Current Noisy Representation

↓

Make It Slightly Cleaner
```

After multiple iterations:

```text
Noise
```

gradually becomes:

```text
Speech Features
```

---

## What Happens During One Diffusion Step?

At a given diffusion step the model has:

```text
Current Noisy Audio Representation

Current Timestep

Conditioning Tensor
```

These are provided to:

```text
DiTDecoder
```

---

## What Happens Inside DiT?

The DiT is composed of:

```text
12 DiT Blocks
```

Execution:

```text
Input Audio Representation

        ↓

DiT Block 1

        ↓

DiT Block 2

        ↓

...

        ↓

DiT Block 12

        ↓

Predicted Velocity
```

---

## What Does The DiT Learn?

The DiT does not directly generate final audio features.

Instead it predicts:

```text
How The Current Representation
Should Be Denoised
```

This output is called:

```text
Velocity Prediction
```

or:

```text
v-prediction
```

---

## How Does Conditioning Influence Generation?

Inside every DiTBlock we have:

```text
Self Attention
```

and

```text
Cross Attention
```

---

### Self Attention

```text
Audio
attends to
Audio
```

Purpose:

```text
Build Internal Audio Structure
```

---

### Cross Attention

```text
Audio
attends to
Conditioning
```

Purpose:

```text
Inject Text Meaning

Inject Speaker Identity

Inject Audio Context
```

---

Conceptually:

```text
Current Audio Representation

          ↓

Ask Conditioning:

"What should I sound like?"

          ↓

Conditioning Provides Guidance
```

This consultation happens:

```text
12 Times Per Diffusion Step
```

and

```text
8 Diffusion Steps Overall
```

meaning conditioning continuously influences generation.

---

## After DiT Finishes

The DiT produces:

```text
Predicted Velocity
```

which is used by the diffusion equations to compute:

```text
pred_x0
```

the model's estimate of clean audio features.

---

The DDPM update then computes:

```text
Next Mean

Next Standard Deviation
```

for the next diffusion step.

---

## Repeat 8 Times

The process:

```text
Predict Denoising Direction

↓

Compute Cleaner Representation

↓

Prepare Next State
```

is repeated:

```text
8 Times
```

---

Progressively:

```text
Noise

 ↓

Rough Speech Structure

 ↓

Cleaner Speech Features

 ↓

Final Audio Features
```

---

## Final Output

After the 8th diffusion step:

```python
[B,100,96]
```

is produced.

These are:

```text
Audio Features
```

not waveform samples.

---

These are passed to:

```text
SoundStream Decoder
```

which performs:

```text
Audio Features

        ↓

Waveform Reconstruction

        ↓

Speech Audio
```

---

## Complete Decoder Story

```text
Conditioning Tensor
(Text + Speaker + Context)

            +

Noise

            ↓

8 Diffusion Steps

Each Step:

    Current Audio Representation
                ↓

           12 DiT Blocks

                ↓

      Predict Denoising Direction

                ↓

      Produce Cleaner Audio State

            ↓

Final Audio Features [B,100,96]

            ↓

SoundStream Decoder

            ↓

Waveform
```

---

## One-Line Summary

VoiceDMDecoder starts from noise and, guided by text, speaker, and context conditioning, repeatedly applies a 12-layer DiT across 8 diffusion steps to progressively denoise the representation until a clean audio feature tensor `[B,100,96]` is generated for waveform reconstruction.
